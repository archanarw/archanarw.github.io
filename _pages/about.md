I'm interested in identifying and modeling the computational principles that underlie goal-directed collaboration, and in building AI systems grounded in those principles. Rather than copying biological mechanisms directly, I want to draw on the principles behind biological collaboration to build collaborative AI.

Concretely, I work on agents that build structured models of other agents as intentional systems with their own goals, beliefs, and abilities; gather information actively to reduce uncertainty about collaborators; and update those beliefs efficiently as new evidence arrives during interaction.

I'm currently a Research Trainee at [Basis](https://www.basis.ai) (New York), advised by [Zenna Tavares](https://www.basis.ai/about/people/zenna-tavares/), where I work on [Project MARA](https://www.basis.ai/blog/mara/)---building AI systems capable of everyday scientific discovery through active experimentation and abstract reasoning. I'll soon be starting as an incoming ELLIS PhD student at TU Darmstadt, advised by [Angela Yu](https://www.psychologie.tu-darmstadt.de/intelligent-systems) in the Computational Modelling of Intelligent Systems lab.

Previously, I completed my Master's in Computer Science at RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau and my Bachelor's in Mathematics and Computing at Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra.

You can find more about my work in my [publications](/al-folio/publications/) and [projects](/al-folio/projects/) pages.
