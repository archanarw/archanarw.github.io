I'm interested in identifying and modeling the computational principles that underlie goal-directed collaboration, and in building AI systems grounded in those principles. Rather than copying biological mechanisms directly, I want to draw on the principles behind biological collaboration to build collaborative AI.

I'm a PhD student at TU Darmstadt, advised by [Angela Yu](https://www.psychologie.tu-darmstadt.de/intelligent-systems) in the Computational Modelling of Intelligent Systems lab.

I was previously a Research Trainee at [Basis](https://www.basis.ai) (New York), advised by [Zenna Tavares](https://www.basis.ai/about/people/zenna-tavares/), where I work on [Project MARA](https://www.basis.ai/blog/mara/)---building AI systems capable of everyday scientific discovery through active experimentation and abstract reasoning.

You can find more about my work on the [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) pages.
